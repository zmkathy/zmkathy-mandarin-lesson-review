# Lesson 1: Greetings & Introductions
## Key Vocabulary
我 | wǒ | I; me
你 | nǐ | you
您 | nín | you, respectful
他 | tā | he; him
她 | tā | she; her
你好 | nǐ hǎo | hello
您好 | nín hǎo | hello, respectful
叫 | jiào | to be called
名字 | míngzi | name
姓 | xìng | surname
贵姓 | guì xìng | honorable surname
认识 | rènshi | to know; meet
老师 | lǎoshī | teacher
学生 | xuésheng | student
朋友 | péngyou | friend
漂亮 | piàoliang | pretty; beautiful
聪明 | cōngming | smart
高 | gāo | tall
幽默 | yōumò | humorous
谢谢 | xièxie | thank you
不客气 | bú kèqi | you're welcome

## Key Sentences
你好！ | nǐ hǎo! | Hello!
您好，老师！ | nín hǎo, lǎoshī! | Hello, teacher!
大家好！ | dàjiā hǎo! | Hello, everyone!
你好吗？ | nǐ hǎo ma? | How are you?
我很好，你呢？ | wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne? | I am very well. How about you?
我也很好。 | wǒ yě hěn hǎo. | I am very well too.
你叫什么名字？ | nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? | What is your name?
我叫…… | wǒ jiào... | My name is...
你姓什么？ | nǐ xìng shénme? | What is your surname?
我姓…… | wǒ xìng... | My surname is...
您贵姓？ | nín guì xìng? | What is your surname? Respectful.
很高兴认识你。 | hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ. | Nice to meet you.
我也很高兴认识你。 | wǒ yě hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ. | Nice to meet you too.
我是学生。 | wǒ shì xuésheng. | I am a student.
他不是老师。 | tā bú shì lǎoshī. | He is not a teacher.
她很漂亮。 | tā hěn piàoliang. | She is very pretty.
他很聪明，也很幽默。 | tā hěn cōngming, yě hěn yōumò. | He is smart and humorous.

# Lesson 2: Age & Chinese Zodiac
## Key Vocabulary
爸爸 | bàba | dad
妈妈 | māma | mom
阿姨 | āyí | aunt; auntie
小朋友 | xiǎo péngyou | child
年轻 | niánqīng | young
多大 | duō dà | how old
出生 | chūshēng | to be born
年纪 | niánjì | age
属 | shǔ | to belong to a zodiac sign
觉得 | juéde | to think; feel
左右 | zuǒyòu | approximately
对 | duì | right; correct

## Key Sentences
这是你妈妈吗？ | zhè shì nǐ māma ma? | Is this your mom?
是，这是我妈妈。 | shì, zhè shì wǒ māma. | Yes, this is my mom.
那是你爸爸吗？ | nà shì nǐ bàba ma? | Is that your dad?
她真年轻。 | tā zhēn niánqīng. | She is really young.
你多大？ | nǐ duō dà? | How old are you?
我二十岁。 | wǒ èrshí suì. | I am twenty years old.
你几岁？ | nǐ jǐ suì? | How old are you? For a child.
您多大年纪？ | nín duō dà niánjì? | How old are you? Respectful.
您高寿？ | nín gāoshòu? | How old are you? A respectful traditional expression for an older person.
你猜，他多大？ | nǐ cāi, tā duō dà? | Guess, how old is he?
我觉得她四十岁左右。 | wǒ juéde tā sìshí suì zuǒyòu. | I think she is about forty.
你是哪年出生的？ | nǐ shì nǎ nián chūshēng de? | What year were you born?
我是一九九八年出生的。 | wǒ shì yī jiǔ jiǔ bā nián chūshēng de. | I was born in 1998.
你属什么？ | nǐ shǔ shénme? | What is your Chinese zodiac sign?
我属龙。 | wǒ shǔ lóng. | I am a Dragon.
她属兔。 | tā shǔ tù. | She is a Rabbit.
对，她属龙。 | duì, tā shǔ lóng. | Right, she is a Dragon.
