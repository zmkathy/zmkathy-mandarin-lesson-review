# Lesson 1: Hello & Self Introduction

## Key Vocabulary
你好 | nǐ hǎo | hello
您好 | nín hǎo | polite hello
你们好 | nǐmen hǎo | hello everyone
我 | wǒ | I; me
叫 | jiào | to be called
是 | shì | am; is; are
名字 | míngzi | name
姓 | xìng | surname
请问 | qǐngwèn | may I ask
贵姓 | guìxìng | your surname, polite
来自 | láizì | come from
加拿大 | Jiānádà | Canada
美国 | Měiguó | United States
中国 | Zhōngguó | China

## Key Sentences
你好！ | nǐ hǎo! | Hello!
我叫 Kathy。 | wǒ jiào Kathy. | My name is Kathy.
你叫什么名字？ | nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? | What is your name?
请问，您贵姓？ | qǐngwèn, nín guìxìng? | May I ask your surname?
我姓 Zhang。 | wǒ xìng Zhang. | My surname is Zhang.
我是中国人。 | wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén. | I am Chinese.
我来自加拿大。 | wǒ láizì Jiānádà. | I am from Canada.
很高兴认识你。 | hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ. | Nice to meet you.
我也很高兴认识你。 | wǒ yě hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ. | Nice to meet you too.
你是哪国人？ | nǐ shì nǎ guó rén? | Which country are you from?
你呢？ | nǐ ne? | How about you?

# Lesson 2: My Family

## Key Vocabulary
家 | jiā | home; family
家人 | jiārén | family members
爸爸 | bàba | dad
妈妈 | māma | mom
哥哥 | gēge | older brother
姐姐 | jiějie | older sister
弟弟 | dìdi | younger brother
妹妹 | mèimei | younger sister
爷爷 | yéye | grandpa
奶奶 | nǎinai | grandma
朋友 | péngyou | friend
谁 | shéi | who
有 | yǒu | have
没有 | méiyǒu | do not have
的 | de | possessive particle
兄弟姐妹 | xiōngdì jiěmèi | siblings
猫 | māo | cat
狗 | gǒu | dog

## Key Sentences
这是谁？ | zhè shì shéi? | Who is this?
这是我的妈妈。 | zhè shì wǒ de māma. | This is my mom.
他是我的哥哥。 | tā shì wǒ de gēge. | He is my older brother.
她是我的妹妹。 | tā shì wǒ de mèimei. | She is my younger sister.
这是你的爸爸吗？ | zhè shì nǐ de bàba ma? | Is this your dad?
我有一个妹妹。 | wǒ yǒu yí ge mèimei. | I have a younger sister.
我没有弟弟。 | wǒ méiyǒu dìdi. | I do not have a younger brother.
他很聪明。 | tā hěn cōngming. | He is smart.
她很友好。 | tā hěn yǒuhǎo. | She is friendly.
它很可爱。 | tā hěn kě'ài. | It is cute.
他很忙。 | tā hěn máng. | He is busy.

# Lesson 3: Daily Plans & Weekly Schedule

## Key Vocabulary

明天 | míngtiān | tomorrow
后天 | hòutiān | the day after tomorrow
上午 | shàngwǔ | morning
中午 | zhōngwǔ | noon; lunchtime
下午 | xiàwǔ | afternoon
晚上 | wǎnshang | evening; night
周末 | zhōumò | weekend
去 | qù | go
跑步 | pǎobù | run
工作 | gōngzuò | work
看电影 | kàn diànyǐng | watch a movie
吃饭 | chīfàn | eat; have a meal
休息 | xiūxi | rest
学中文 | xué Zhōngwén | study Chinese
机场 | jīchǎng | airport
超市 | chāoshì | supermarket
公园 | gōngyuán | park
星期一 | xīngqī yī | Monday
星期天 | xīngqī tiān | Sunday

## Key Sentences
今天上午我去跑步。 | jīntiān shàngwǔ wǒ qù pǎobù. | This morning, I go running.
明天下午我去工作。 | míngtiān xiàwǔ wǒ qù gōngzuò. | Tomorrow afternoon, I go to work.
今天晚上我去看电影。 | jīntiān wǎnshang wǒ qù kàn diànyǐng. | This evening, I go watch a movie.
后天晚上我去休息。 | hòutiān wǎnshang wǒ qù xiūxi. | The day after tomorrow evening, I go rest.
星期六上午我去跑步。 | xīngqīliù shàngwǔ wǒ qù pǎobù. | Saturday morning, I go running.
你明天做什么？ | nǐ míngtiān zuò shénme? | What are you doing tomorrow?
明天下午呢？ | míngtiān xiàwǔ ne? | What about tomorrow afternoon?
我去机场。 | wǒ qù jīchǎng. | I go to the airport.

# Lesson 4: Numbers & How Old Are You?

## Key Vocabulary
零 | líng | zero
一 | yī | one
二 | èr | two
两 | liǎng | two, before measure words
三 | sān | three
四 | sì | four
五 | wǔ | five
六 | liù | six
七 | qī | seven
八 | bā | eight
九 | jiǔ | nine
十 | shí | ten
一百 | yì bǎi | one hundred
一千 | yì qiān | one thousand
一万 | yí wàn | ten thousand
岁 | suì | years old
今年 | jīnnián | this year
多少 | duōshǎo | how many; how much
几 | jǐ | how many, usually small number
个 | gè | general measure word

## Key Sentences
你今年多少岁了？ | nǐ jīnnián duōshǎo suì le? | How old are you this year?
你多大了？ | nǐ duōdà le? | How old are you this year?
你今年几岁？ | nǐ jīnnián jǐ suì? | How old are you this year?
我二十八岁。 | wǒ èrshíbā suì. | I am 28.
我今年二十八岁了。 | wǒ jīnnián èrshíbā suì le. | I am 28 this year.
他三十五岁。 | tā sānshíwǔ suì. | He is 35.
她四十八岁。 | tā sìshíbā suì. | She is 48.
你家有几个人？ | nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ ge rén? | How many people are in your family?
我家有四个人。 | wǒ jiā yǒu sì ge rén. | There are four people in my family.
你妈妈多少岁？ | nǐ māma duōshǎo suì? | How old is your mom?
我妈妈五十五岁。 | wǒ māma wǔshíwǔ suì. | My mom is 55.

# Lesson 5: Dates & Time

## Key Vocabulary
日 | rì | date
年 | nián | year
月 | yuè | month
号 | hào | date; day of month
星期 | xīngqī | weekday
生日 | shēngrì | birthday
时间 | shíjiān | time
点 | diǎn | o'clock
半 | bàn | half
一刻 | yí kè | quarter
分 | fēn | minute
几点 | jǐ diǎn | what time
现在 | xiànzài | now
早上 | zǎoshang | early morning
两点 | liǎng diǎn | two o'clock

## Key Sentences
今天是几月几号？ | jīntiān shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào? | What is today's date?
今天是六月二十八号。 | jīntiān shì liù yuè èrshíbā hào. | Today is June 28.
今天星期几？ | jīntiān xīngqī jǐ? | What day is today?
今天星期三。 | jīntiān xīngqī sān. | Today is Wednesday.
你的生日是几月几号？ | nǐ de shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào? | When is your birthday?
我的生日是六月二十八号。 | wǒ de shēngrì shì liù yuè èrshíbā hào. | My birthday is June 28.
几点了？ | jǐ diǎn le? | What time is it?
现在三点半。 | xiànzài sān diǎn bàn. | It is 3:30 now.
现在晚上十一点十五分了。 | xiànzài wǎnshang shíyī diǎn shíwǔ fēn le. | It is 11:15 PM now.
早上九点零八分。 | zǎoshang jiǔ diǎn líng bā fēn. | 9:08 AM.

# Lesson 6: Shopping Basics

## Key Vocabulary
钱 | qián | money
人民币 | rénmínbì | RMB; Chinese currency
元 | yuán | formal RMB unit
块 | kuài | spoken RMB unit
毛 | máo | tenth of a yuan
现金 | xiànjīn | cash
刷卡 | shuākǎ | pay by card
扫码 | sǎomǎ | scan to pay
苹果 | píngguǒ | apple
水 | shuǐ | water
杯子 | bēizi | cup
车票 | chēpiào | ticket
纪念品 | jìniànpǐn | souvenir
买 | mǎi | buy
想买 | xiǎng mǎi | want to buy
这个 | zhège | this one
那个 | nàge | that one
多少钱 | duōshǎo qián | how much money
一共 | yígòng | total; altogether

## Key Sentences
你想买什么？ | nǐ xiǎng mǎi shénme? | What do you want to buy?
我想买苹果。 | wǒ xiǎng mǎi píngguǒ. | I want to buy apples.
我想买水。 | wǒ xiǎng mǎi shuǐ. | I want to buy water.
这个多少钱？ | zhège duōshǎo qián? | How much is this?
那个多少钱？ | nàge duōshǎo qián? | How much is that?
八块。 | bā kuài. | Eight yuan.
十八块五。 | shíbā kuài wǔ. | 18.5 yuan.
一共多少钱？ | yígòng duōshǎo qián? | How much in total?
一共三十块。 | yígòng sānshí kuài. | 30 yuan in total.
太贵了。 | tài guì le. | Too expensive.
可以刷卡吗？ | kěyǐ shuākǎ ma? | Can I pay by card?
可以扫码吗？ | kěyǐ sǎomǎ ma? | Can I scan to pay?
可以用现金吗？ | kěyǐ yòng xiànjīn ma? | Can I use cash?
可以。 | kěyǐ. | Yes, you can.
不可以。 | bù kěyǐ. | No, you can't.
