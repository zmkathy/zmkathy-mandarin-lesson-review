# Lesson 1: Hello & Self Introduction

## Key Vocabulary
你好 | nǐ hǎo | hello
您好 | nín hǎo | polite hello
你们好 | nǐmen hǎo | hello everyone
我 | wǒ | I; me
叫 | jiào | to be called
是 | shì | am; is; are
名字 | míngzi | name
姓 | xìng | surname
请问 | qǐngwèn | may I ask
贵姓 | guìxìng | your surname, polite
来自 | láizì | come from
加拿大 | Jiānádà | Canada
美国 | Měiguó | United States
中国 | Zhōngguó | China

## Key Sentences
你好！ | nǐ hǎo! | Hello!
我叫 Kathy。 | wǒ jiào Kathy. | My name is Kathy.
你叫什么名字？ | nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? | What is your name?
请问，您贵姓？ | qǐngwèn, nín guìxìng? | May I ask your surname?
我姓 Zhang。 | wǒ xìng Zhang. | My surname is Zhang.
我是中国人。 | wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén. | I am Chinese.
我来自加拿大。 | wǒ láizì Jiānádà. | I am from Canada.
很高兴认识你。 | hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ. | Nice to meet you.
我也很高兴认识你。 | wǒ yě hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ. | Nice to meet you too.
你是哪国人？ | nǐ shì nǎ guó rén? | Which country are you from?
你呢？ | nǐ ne? | How about you?

## Key Sentences
今天是几月几号？ | jīntiān shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào? | What is today's date?

我的生日是六月二十八号。 | wǒ de shēngrì shì liù yuè èrshíbā hào. | My birthday is June 28.
几点了？ | jǐ diǎn le? | What time is it?
现在三点半。 | xiànzài sān diǎn bàn. | It is 3:30 now.
现在晚上十一点十五分了。 | xiànzài wǎnshang shíyī diǎn shíwǔ fēn le. | It is 11:15 PM now.

# Lesson 10: Asking for Directions

## Key Vocabulary
前面 | qiánmiàn | in front; ahead
后面 | hòumiàn | behind

右边 | yòubiān | right side
旁边 | pángbiān | beside; next to
走 | zǒu | walk; go
一直 | yìzhí | straight; continuously
左拐 | zuǒ guǎi | turn left
右拐 | yòu guǎi | turn right
然后 | ránhòu | then
向 | xiàng | towards
远 | yuǎn | far
近 | jìn | near

## Key Sentences
请问，地铁站怎么走？ | qǐngwèn, dìtiězhàn zěnme zǒu? | Excuse me, how do I get to the metro station?

一直走，然后右拐。 | yìzhí zǒu, ránhòu yòu guǎi. | Go straight, then turn right.
地铁站在酒店的前面。 | dìtiězhàn zài jiǔdiàn de qiánmiàn. | The metro station is in front of the hotel.
咖啡店在酒店的左边。 | kāfēidiàn zài jiǔdiàn de zuǒbiān. | The café is on the left of the hotel.
地铁站在哪儿？ | dìtiězhàn zài nǎr? | Where is the metro station?
远吗？ | yuǎn ma? | Is it far?
不远，很近。 | bù yuǎn, hěn jìn. | Not far, very near.
